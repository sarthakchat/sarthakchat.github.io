**Publication:** Microsoft  
**Date:** July 30, 2024

**Feature Article:** Research on enhancing EV charging infrastructure conducted at Georgia Tech was featured in a Microsoft article titled "Leveraging AI to Enhance EV Charging Infrastructure." I contributed to this research during my graduate studies.

**Key Insights:**

The team used **Microsoft's Azure OpenAI Service** to analyze EV charging behavior in **72 languages**, revealing:

- **Data Scale Breakthrough:** Processing user reviews that would have taken human experts 99 weeks to analyze manually.

- **Consumer Experience Analysis:** Identifying key issues affecting charging station reliability and pricing transparency.

- **AI Model Innovation:** Developing tailored models that outperformed human experts in both speed and accuracy.

- **Policy Relevance:** Creating data-driven recommendations for government intervention to improve infrastructure reliability and standardize pricing.

The research team's work was published at the AAAI Fall Symposium on Climate Change and AI, demonstrating how generative AI can extract pricing mechanisms from operator descriptions with high accuracy at reduced costs.

**Read More:** [Leveraging AI to Enhance EV Charging Infrastructure](https://www.microsoft.com/en/customers/story/1800038623596257192-gatech-azure-ai-studio-higher-education-en-united-states)

**Publication:** Microsoft

🏛️ **Feature Article:** Our lab's research on enhancing electric vehicle (EV) charging infrastructure, conducted under the guidance of Dr. Omar Asensio at Georgia Tech, was featured in a Microsoft article titled "**Leveraging AI to Enhance EV Charging Infrastructure**." As a contributing member of the research team, I played a significant role in this project.

🔍 **Key Insights:**

Our research utilized **Microsoft’s Azure OpenAI Service** to analyze global EV charging behavior in **72 languages**, unlocking valuable insights for policy development and infrastructure improvement. The collaboration allowed us to:

- **Analyze Unstructured Data at Scale:** By tapping into the global community of EV drivers' reviews and comments, we processed vast amounts of unstructured data that would have taken human experts 99 weeks to analyze manually.

- **Enhance Consumer Confidence:** We addressed the critical issue of consumer confidence in public charging stations, identifying factors contributing to negative experiences, such as charger unreliability and inconsistent pricing.

- **Develop Scalable AI Solutions:** Through training and fine-tuning AI models with context prompts and specific examples, we achieved rapid data classification and predictive modeling, outperforming human experts in both speed and accuracy.

- **Impact Policy and Infrastructure:** Our findings highlight the need for government intervention to improve charger reliability, standardize pricing, and enhance interoperability. The research contributes to environmental sustainability and equitable access to EV technology.

💡 **My Contribution:**

As a key contributor to this project, my responsibilities included:

- **AI Model Development:** Assisting in training and refining the AI models used for data analysis, enabling them to understand domain-specific language and sentiments expressed by EV drivers globally.

- **Data Analysis:** Participating in the analysis of EV charging behavior data across multiple regions, including the U.S.

- **Research Publication:** Co-authoring our recent publication at the AAAI Fall Symposium on Climate Change and AI, where we demonstrated that generative AI models can effectively extract pricing mechanisms from operator descriptions with high accuracy and at substantially lower costs.
