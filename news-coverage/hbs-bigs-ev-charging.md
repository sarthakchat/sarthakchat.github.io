**Publication:** Harvard Business School Institute for Business in Global Society  
**Date:** June 26, 2024

**Feature Article:** Research on electric vehicle charging infrastructure was featured in an article by the Harvard Business School's Institute for Business in Global Society titled "The state of EV charging in America: Harvard research shows chargers 78% reliable and pricing like the 'Wild West'."

**Key Findings:**

This study examined over **1 million EV charging station reviews** across North America, Europe, and Asia. Using AI analysis, the research identified several key challenges:

- **Reliability Issues:** U.S. public charging stations average only 78% reliability, with approximately one in five charging attempts failing.

- **Pricing Confusion:** EV drivers face inconsistent and non-transparent pricing models, making cost comparison difficult.

- **Infrastructure Gaps:** "Charging deserts" exist in rural and certain urban areas, creating equity concerns and barriers to adoption.

- **User Experience:** Problems such as ICE vehicles blocking stations and poor maintenance significantly impact driver satisfaction.

**Implications:**

The research identifies opportunities for business innovation in reliability solutions and maintenance services, while providing policymakers with data to develop more effective regulations for standardizing and expanding charging networks.
