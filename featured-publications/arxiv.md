title: "RAG vs. Long Context: Examining Frontier LLMs for Environmental Review Document Comprehension"
---

**Publication:** arXiv preprint  
**Date:** July 10, 2024

**Paper Overview:** This study evaluates how frontier large language models perform when comprehending environmental impact statements (EIS) and other lengthy environmental review documents.

**Key Findings:**
- Created NEPAQuAD1.0, the first benchmark for evaluating LLMs on EIS document question-answering
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) models significantly outperformed long context models
- Performance varied by question type, with better results on closed questions versus problem-solving ones

**Significance:** This work addresses practical challenges in using AI to assist with environmental review processes. The findings offer guidance for implementing LLMs in regulatory contexts, suggesting RAG as a promising approach for processing long, domain-specific documents like environmental impact statements.

**Access the Paper:** [Full paper on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.07321)
