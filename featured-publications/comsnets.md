**Publication:** International Conference on Communication Systems & Networks (COMSNETS)  
**Date:** January 5, 2021

**Paper Overview:** This research presents a novel method for estimating traffic states using Dedicated Short-Range Communications (DSRC) enabled probe vehicles.

**Key Findings:**
- Developed algorithms for real-time traffic state estimation using limited probe vehicle data
- Demonstrated improved accuracy compared to traditional traffic monitoring systems
- Evaluated performance across various traffic density scenarios and communication ranges

**Significance:** The approach enables more reliable traffic monitoring without extensive sensor infrastructure, supporting intelligent transportation systems and smarter city planning. This method offers cost-effective alternatives to traditional traffic monitoring while providing higher temporal resolution data.
