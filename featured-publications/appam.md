**Publication:** 2023 APPAM Fall Research Conference  
**Date:** November 9, 2023

**Study Overview:** This research demonstrates how large language models can analyze consumer responses to electric vehicle charging pricing schemes, providing valuable insights for policymakers.

**Key Findings:**
- LLMs effectively classify consumer sentiment toward various charging pricing models
- Zero and few-shot learning capabilities enable analysis with minimal training data
- Method provides cost-effective tool for real-time assessment of infrastructure issues

**Significance:** The research offers a novel approach to understanding how pricing impacts consumer adoption of clean energy technologies. By leveraging LLMs, policymakers can gain timely insights into consumer behavior in the rapidly evolving EV charging market, helping identify barriers to widespread adoption.
