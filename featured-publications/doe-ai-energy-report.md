**Publication:** U.S. Department of Energy  
**Date:** April 29, 2024

**Report Overview:** The report "AI and Energy: Opportunities for a Modern Grid and Clean Energy Economy" was highlighted in a Department of Energy announcement on new actions to enhance America's global leadership in Artificial Intelligence. 

**Key Focus Areas:**
- Potential of AI to deliver clean, affordable, and resilient electric power
- Application of AI technologies in building a modern clean energy economy
- Strategic approaches for responsible AI integration in energy systems

**Policy Context:** This report was developed as part of the DOE's response to President Biden's Executive Order on the Safe, Secure, and Trustworthy Development and Use of Artificial Intelligence.

**Significance:** As the first report of its kind from the DOE, it examines AI's near-term potential in supporting America's clean energy economy. Its inclusion in the Department's high-level announcement reflects the growing importance of AI applications in addressing energy sector challenges.

The report serves as a resource for understanding how AI can be applied to solve practical challenges in energy infrastructure development and environmental policy, contributing to national goals of accelerating clean energy deployment.
