**Publication:** Conference of Transportation Research Group of India  
**Date:** December 15, 2022

**Paper Overview:** This research explores advanced data imputation techniques to improve traffic state estimation and prediction using Wi-Fi sensor networks.

**Key Findings:**
- Developed novel imputation algorithms addressing missing data in Wi-Fi traffic sensors
- Compared performance across multiple imputation methods in various traffic conditions
- Demonstrated significant improvements in estimation accuracy under partial data loss scenarios

**Significance:** This work addresses a critical challenge in urban traffic monitoring systems: handling incomplete data from inexpensive sensor networks. The proposed methods enable more reliable traffic state estimation with lower infrastructure costs, supporting the development of smarter transportation systems in resource-constrained environments.
