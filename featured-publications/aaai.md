title: "A Generative AI Approach to Pricing Mechanisms and Consumer Behavior in the EV Charging Market"
---

**Publication:** AAAI Symposium Series  
**Date:** March 15, 2024

**Study Overview:** This research demonstrates how large language models (LLMs) can evaluate online consumer reviews of EV charging stations to extract insights about pricing mechanisms.

**Key Findings:**
- Achieved 92.00% classification accuracy and 0.931 F1 score with GPT-3
- GPT-4 attained comparable performance without any training examples
- Identified relationship between pricing scheme complexity and consumer sentiment

**Significance:** This work provides a cost-effective method for policymakers to gain real-time insights into pricing impacts and emerging business models in the clean energy transition. The approach enables analysis of consumer behavior in the rapidly growing EV charging market without requiring expensive traditional surveys or focus groups.
