Sarthak is an Energy Data Scientist at the U.S. Department of Energy's Pacific Northwest National Laboratory (PNNL), specializing in developing advanced artificial intelligence solutions for energy systems and environmental sustainability. His work focuses on applying machine learning and data analytics to optimize power grid operations, enhance decarbonization strategies, and advance sustainable energy infrastructure.

At PNNL, Sarthak collaborates with multiple Department of Energy offices, including the Office of Policy, the Advanced Research Projects Agency–Energy (ARPA-E), and the Office of Energy Efficiency and Renewable Energy (EERE). He leads projects that involve creating AI-based tools for power grid analysis, automating power system simulations to boost operational efficiency, and developing decision-support systems that integrate climate modeling with transportation planning. Additionally, he plays a key role in developing AI tools to streamline permitting processes and improve siting for clean energy infrastructure. Through these initiatives, he contributes significantly to national goals in energy efficiency and environmental sustainability.

As a key team member of the PolicyAI initiative, Sarthak contributed to efforts that were featured in a White House report highlighting significant AI actions taken 180 days following President Biden’s landmark Executive Order on AI. This recognition underscores his commitment to leveraging artificial intelligence for informed policy-making and advancing national interests.

During his graduate studies at the Georgia Institute of Technology, Sarthak's research on electric vehicle infrastructure and energy policy analysis gained notable attention. His work was featured by Harvard Business School’s Business and Government Policy (BiGS) Institute, Microsoft, and The New York Times .

Building on his expertise in machine learning and energy systems, Sarthak continues to drive innovation in the energy sector, striving to contribute to a more sustainable and efficient energy future.
