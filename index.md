Sarthak is an Energy Data Scientist at the U.S. Department of Energy’s Pacific Northwest National Laboratory (PNNL), specializing in developing artificial intelligence solutions for energy systems and environmental sustainability. His work focuses on applying machine learning and data analytics techniques to optimize power grid operations, enhance decarbonization strategies, and advance sustainable energy infrastructure.

At PNNL, Sarthak collaborates with various offices within the Department of Energy, including the Grid Deployment Office, the Office of Policy, the Advanced Research Projects Agency–Energy (ARPA-E), and the Office of Energy Efficiency and Renewable Energy (EERE). He leads projects that involve creating AI-based tools for power grid analysis, automating power system simulations to boost operational efficiency, and developing decision-support systems to optimize the use of alternative fuels in freight transportation. Additionally, he plays a key role in developing AI tools to streamline permitting processes and improve site selection for clean energy infrastructure. Through these initiatives, he contributes significantly to national goals in energy efficiency and environmental sustainability.

As a key team member of the PolicyAI initiative, Sarthak contributed to efforts featured in a White House report that highlighted significant AI actions taken 180 days after President Biden’s landmark Executive Order on AI. This recognition underscores his commitment to leveraging artificial intelligence for informed policymaking and advancing national interests.

During his graduate studies at the Georgia Institute of Technology, Sarthak’s research on electric vehicle infrastructure and energy policy analysis garnered notable attention. His work was featured by Harvard Business School’s Business and Government Policy (BiGS) Institute, Microsoft, and The New York Times.

Building on his expertise in machine learning and energy systems, Sarthak continues to drive innovation in the energy sector, striving to contribute to a more sustainable and efficient energy future.
